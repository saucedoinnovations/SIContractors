How Email Works

This app uses EmailJS — a free service that lets you send emails directly from your browser using your own Gmail or email account. No backend server needed.

Free tier: 200 emails/month — more than enough for most contractors.

1️⃣
Go to emailjs.com and create a free account
2️⃣
Add your Gmail as an Email Service, get your Service ID
3️⃣
Create an Email Template, get your Template ID & Public Key

Your EmailJS template should use these variables: {{to_email}} {{to_name}} {{from_name}} {{estimate_number}} {{estimate_total}} {{estimate_link}} {{message}}

📡 View Tracking (Pipedream)

When a client opens their estimate link, EstimatePro pings your Pipedream webhook — automatically marking the estimate as Viewed on your dashboard and sending you a notification email.

1️⃣
Go to pipedream.com → create free account
2️⃣
New Workflow → trigger: HTTP / Webhook → copy the URL
3️⃣
Add Send Email step to notify yourself → paste URL below
